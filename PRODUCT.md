# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Public-sector decision-makers, mostly 40 to 65: sheriffs and undersheriffs, jail commanders and compliance lieutenants, correctional health services administrators, county counsel and risk managers, county administrators and procurement officers, state corrections health leadership. They arrive after a referral, a conference contact or an RFP response, on a desktop inside a county network (often a locked-down or older browser), to check whether the firm is credible. Several people review the site during one long sales cycle. Mobile is for a quick look at a conference.

Job to be done: decide in seconds whether these people know correctional healthcare and can be trusted, then find services, team credentials, the capability statement or a way to start a conversation in one or two clicks.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Actura Health, an independent correctional healthcare consulting firm: the oversight layer between a jail or prison and its healthcare vendor. The firm does not deliver care. Credibility first, qualified leads second. Success: every page indexed, page 1 for "NCCHC accreditation consulting" and "correctional healthcare quality assurance consulting", 2 to 4 qualified inquiries per quarter, client edits content without a developer.

## Brand Personality

Independent, credible, collaborative. A firm county counsel would trust, not a startup. Plain, exact, calm. Proof by role and credential, never by name. Commitments instead of guarantees. Visitors should feel reassured and confident.

References named by the client: Chartis (healthcare advisory credibility, clean structure), Stripe (explains complex things clearly).

## Anti-references

- Startup or SaaS voice and visuals.
- Corrections cliches: bars, handcuffs, orange jumpsuits. Never an identifiable incarcerated person.
- Generic consulting copy ("transforming", "comprehensive solutions").
- Invented numbers, invented testimonials, client logos, the NCCHC logo.
- Care vendor look (patient-first lifestyle photography): Actura is not a provider.

## Design Principles

1. Independence is shown as a structural fact (what the firm does not sell), not claimed as an adjective.
2. Evidence over assertion: every visual and line of copy should look like something that would hold up in front of a board or a court.
3. Readable first: large type, strong contrast, nothing that depends on JavaScript or a modern browser.
4. One or two clicks to anything a reviewer needs. No dead ends, no gated downloads.
5. Unconfirmed facts are flagged in the UI, never guessed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Older audience: body 20px, small text floor 16px, strong contrast. Full keyboard and screen-reader support. Reduced-motion fallback. Every page works with JavaScript off and on older Chromium, Edge and Firefox ESR.
